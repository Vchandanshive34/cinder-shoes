01 — Technology

The rocker does the work

The apex sits at 62% of last length — far enough forward that the shoe tips you into toe-off instead of asking your calf to do it. The heel is bevelled at 18° so a heel-first landing never fights the geometry.

02 — Build

Four layers, one spring

The plate is sandwiched at 38% of foam height — low enough to resist bending, high enough that the top layer still compresses under the forefoot. The callouts on the shoe mark each layer.
